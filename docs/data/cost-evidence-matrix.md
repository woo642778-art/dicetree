## What public research did and did not establish

Current official Random Dice 2 store pages confirm customizable growth paths but do not publish a rank-by-rank Dice Tree cost table. Searches across current Korean community indexing did not surface a trustworthy full 1→50 or 1→100 cost ladder for Random Dice 2. Search results frequently mix in Random Dice: Defense upgrade tables from 2020–2025; those tables are explicitly excluded from V2.

Therefore the planner uses `observed next cost` rather than extrapolating a single cost across every remaining rank. Once another current screenshot shows the same node at a different rank, that pair can be added independently and the site can reconstruct the true ladder from evidence.
